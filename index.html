Edison Home privacy notice

Last updated October 24, 2026

Operator and purpose

Edison Home is a private development integration operated by Building Technologies Inc. for the owner's approved Edison devices and locations. It connects an Alexa conversation to the owner's local Edison installation. It is not currently a publicly distributed Alexa skill.

Account linking and permission

Sign-in uses Amazon's official Login with Amazon flow. Edison does not ask for or receive your Amazon password. The requested profile:user_id permission identifies the linked Amazon account; it does not request your name, email, postal address, contacts, purchases or payment information. Linking an Amazon account does not automatically grant access to Edison. The local enrolled owner must approve the particular linked account and Alexa device, allowed locations, scopes and expiration. Conversation permission does not authorize weather, knowledge, camera, home or desktop actions.

Information processed

Amazon sends the skill an utterance transcript, request/session identifiers, Alexa account and device identifiers, and the linked account's access token. Edison verifies the Amazon request and validates the token with Amazon. The local grant store uses hashes of account and device identifiers to bind the owner's approval. Tokens are used in memory and are not written to Edison's source, request logs or conversation records.

For an authorized conversation, the local Edison installation sends the

question and its bounded conversation context to its configured OpenAI voice provider. It returns the answer transcript to Amazon for Alexa speech. Provider-generated audio is discarded by this integration. The integration does not capture the PC microphone or upload permanent provider credentials. Amazon and the configured model provider process information under their respective policies; this notice does not change those policies.

Transport and storage

A separately approved HTTPS relay carries authorized requests and replies. If the temporary Cloudflare relay is selected, Cloudflare provides that transport. The relay does not expose the existing Edison or Sandra web APIs. While the relay is running, its cleanup routine deletes temporary broker records whose response deadline ended more than five minutes ago. If the PC or service is stopped or suspended, cleanup waits until the service runs again. This is not a guarantee that all copies, backups or other providers' records are deleted within five minutes. Edison's local property conversation history remains under the existing installation's history settings and can outlast the temporary broker record. No microphone recording or generated-audio file is created by this relay. Local approval records are protected with the enrolled Windows owner's DPAPI storage. Expired or revoked approvals cannot authorize later requests.

Displays and other capabilities

The current display preview contains the Edison avatar and synthetic setup status. Weather, approved knowledge and camera status need separate owner permission and an authenticated, location-bound provider. Camera video is currently unavailable through this integration. No camera stream, private footage, device credentials, broad Sandra harness export, home control or desktop control is made public by this setup.

Stopping and revoking access

Say stop to end the current Edison Alexa session. The local owner can revoke a device/account grant; unlinking the skill in the Alexa app removes its account linkage. Stop, unlink and revoke do not themselves delete previously stored local conversation history. Contact the Edison owner/operator through your existing support channel for help with local history or approval records.

Deployment status

This notice describes the prepared private integration. Account-linking setup, the public relay, device enrollment and physical Echo acceptance are not yet complete. It must be reviewed again if public distribution, camera video, additional data scopes or hosting/storage behavior changes.
